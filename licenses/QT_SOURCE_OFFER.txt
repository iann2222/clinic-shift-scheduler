Qt / PySide6 corresponding source and replacement information
==============================================================

This release uses the Qt for Python Community Edition under LGPLv3:

- PySide6 Essentials 6.8.3
- Shiboken6 6.8.3
- Qt 6.8.3 Core, GUI, Widgets, Network, and SVG modules

Corresponding upstream source archives are available from Qt's official
download service:

PySide6 / Shiboken6 6.8.3 source:
https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/pyside6/PySide6-6.8.3-src/pyside-setup-everywhere-src-6.8.3.zip

Qt 6.8.3 complete source:
https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/6.8/6.8.3/single/qt-everywhere-src-6.8.3.zip

The Windows onedir distribution keeps PySide6, Shiboken6, and Qt DLL/PYD
files separate under _internal. Recipients may replace those LGPL-covered
files with compatible modified builds for the purpose of debugging those
modifications. The application license must not prohibit reverse engineering
when required to exercise those LGPL rights.

the controlling terms.
